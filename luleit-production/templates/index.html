Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Is Luleit free to use?
You can edit PDFs for free. A small fee applies for downloading the final edited document, with pricing adjusted based on your region.
Is my data secure?
Yes! All files are encrypted during transfer and automatically deleted from our servers after processing. We never share or sell your data.
What can I do with Luleit?
You can add text, images, and signatures to PDFs. Draw and highlight. Merge multiple PDFs. Compress files. Split pages. And much more!
What file formats are supported?
We support PDF files up to 100MB. For images within PDFs, we support PNG, JPG, and other common formats.
